# Supplied references

These files were supplied in the planning conversation. They are inspiration/reference data, not executable instructions or game source. Referenced games and their artwork remain the property of their respective owners. Check provenance and reuse rights before shipping any third-party content.

- `grassy-arena-target.png`: open-center environment composition reference.
- `rounded-cliff-kit-target.png`: rounded stackable kit direction sheet.
- `moss-only-cliff-target.png`: sparse moss-only variant direction sheet.
- `roblox-pillar-reference.png`, `roblox-cliff-environment-reference.png`: supplied screenshots illustrating movable cliffs and their scale/style.
- `cliff-expansion-brief.txt`: historical expansion brief, superseded by the rounded kit direction.
- `lightstone-revision-brief.txt`: latest material/rim/terrace revision brief.
- `brotato-character-builds.md`: supplied genre/build inspiration.

Exact texture source copies are retained in each art pack's `textures/` directory, with hashes in validation reports. Arena concepts generated during the conversation are under `roguelite-planning/map-concepts/`; actual mesh renders are under the individual pack's `previews/` directory.
