# Brotato Character Build Reference

Strengths, weaknesses, and the top community build for every character on [brotato-builds.com](https://brotato-builds.com/characters). Compiled so you don't have to click through all 63 pages yourself.

---

## Well Rounded
**Unlock** Default

**Strengths**
- Very forgiving early game from bonus Max HP and speed
- Free Harvesting from the start powers a strong economy
- Flexible, works with melee or ranged setups
- Great character for learning the game's fundamentals

**Weaknesses**
- No explosive passive, lower power ceiling than specialists
- Needs good stat scaling and items to keep up at high difficulty
- No built-in damage multiplier, so boss/elite kills can be slow

**Signature build** Beginner's SMG (SMG + Double Barrel Shotgun)

---

## Brawler
**Unlock** Default

**Strengths**
- Huge attack speed bonus with Unarmed weapons for very high DPS
- Starts with a Fist and bonus Dodge for an aggressive early game
- Scales extremely well with dodge and attack speed items

**Weaknesses**
- Severe Range and Ranged Damage penalties lock you into melee
- Short-range playstyle is risky without regen, armor and HP
- Depends heavily on finding the right Unarmed weapons and dodge items

**Signature build** Speed Demon Brawler (6x Claw)

---

## Crazy
**Unlock** Default

**Strengths**
- Strong synergy with Precise weapons thanks to bonus range
- Free Knife and +25% Attack Speed give a powerful early game
- Scales very well with Crit Chance and Melee/Ranged Damage
- Works with both melee (Knife) and ranged (Shuriken) builds

**Weaknesses**
- -30% Dodge makes defensive scaling harder
- -10 Ranged Damage weakens generic (non-Precise) ranged weapons
- Fragile, relies on killing threats before they close the distance
- Struggles without committing to crit-focused Precise weapons

**Signature build** Knife Pro (6x Knife)

---

## Ranger
**Unlock** Default

**Strengths**
- High base range and strong ranged damage scaling
- Starts with a Pistol, online from wave 1
- Excellent with piercing and multi-projectile ranged weapons
- Flexible weapon pool (pistols, SMGs, shotguns, precision guns)

**Weaknesses**
- Cannot equip melee weapons at all
- Max HP scaling is reduced, making him naturally squishier
- Needs real investment in armor/dodge to survive elites and bosses
- Positioning mistakes are punished heavily due to low effective HP

**Signature build** Range Damage Ranger (Revolver/SMG/Pistol)

---

## Mage
**Unlock** Default

**Strengths**
- Huge scaling from Elemental Damage (increased modification bonus)
- Starts with Snake and Scared Sausage for burning synergies from wave 1
- Excellent at clearing big waves once burn and AoE are online
- Works well with a wide range of elemental weapons and burn items

**Weaknesses**
- Melee and Ranged Damage are basically unusable (-100% modifications)
- Engineering scaling is heavily reduced
- Needs specific elemental items to reach full potential
- Can be vulnerable on higher waves without enough armor/regen/sustain

**Signature build** Taser Wizard (6x Taser)

---

## Beast Master
**Unlock** Unlock Bonk Dog, Catling Gun, Bot-O-Mine, Blazemander, and Lootworm

**Strengths**
- Huge scaling, all pets benefit from Melee/Ranged/Elemental/Engineering
- Free Lootworm from wave 1 gives strong economy and auto pickup
- High Speed from pets lets you outrun most enemies
- +10 Luck early makes it easier to find key pets and rares
- Can reach a point where pets kill enemies as soon as they spawn

**Weaknesses**
- No weapons at all, relies entirely on pet AI and positioning
- Early waves are fragile until you find more pets and basic defenses
- Can struggle to focus a single boss if pets are spread out
- Screen gets visually chaotic, making dodging harder
- Heavily item-dependent, bad shop luck on pets can brick a run

**Signature build** Pet-Swarm Summoner (no weapons, full pet roster)

---

## Chunky
**Unlock** Die for the first time

**Strengths**
- Excellent HP scaling, every point of Max HP boosts survivability and damage
- Consumables are very strong (bonus healing, Extra Stomach synergy)
- Max HP modifications are increased
- Naturally suits explosive/AoE builds that reward facetanking

**Weaknesses**
- Speed modification penalty makes him extremely slow
- No life steal access, and reduced HP regen/dodge scaling
- Needs disciplined item picks to avoid HP caps and armor loss
- Mid-game is dangerous before HP/armor/damage come fully online

**Signature build** MukBoomer (5x Chopper, consumable/explosive HP scaling)

---

## Old
**Unlock** Kill 300 enemies

**Strengths**
- Innate enemy speed reduction makes waves safer and easier to read
- Smaller map and fewer enemies create a more controlled battlefield
- Extra Harvesting helps the economy without chasing materials
- Great synergy with stand-still builds and defensive melee weapons

**Weaknesses**
- Base Speed penalty makes dodging and repositioning harder
- Lower enemy count reduces kill-based income and on-kill effects
- Needs patience and good positioning, chasing enemies is punished
- Can struggle against ranged elites without enough defense

**Signature build** Tanky Rock Commander (6x Rock, stand-still tank)

---

## Lucky
**Unlock** Collect 300 materials

**Strengths**
- Extremely high starting Luck and amplified Luck scaling
- Material-pickup passive adds free chip damage (Cyberball/Baby Elephant synergy)
- Great synergy with ricochet and multi-hit ranged/curse weapons
- Can scale into powerful Endless or high-difficulty builds via Luck items

**Weaknesses**
- -60% Attack Speed makes the early game fragile
- -50% XP Gain slows leveling, forcing reliance on shop items
- Very dependent on getting key Luck/pickup items, bad rolls can stall a run
- Needs careful defensive investment since so much goes into economy/damage

**Signature build** Slingshot Lucky (6x Slingshot)

---

## Mutant
**Unlock** Kill 2000 enemies

**Strengths**
- Levels up extremely fast (-66% XP required)
- Can stack many level-up stats and XP-scaling effects for late-game power
- Synergizes very well with Quarterstaff and % XP Gain items
- Flexible stat distribution across many level-ups

**Weaknesses**
- +50% Items Price makes shops weak and punishes careless rerolling
- Economy/item power spikes are slower and more expensive
- Greedy XP or curse-focused lines can make waves very dangerous
- Needs good defensive planning for high-curse, high-enemy waves

**Signature build** Newcomers Mutant Build (Double Barrel Shotgun + Pistols)

---

## Generalist
**Unlock** Collect 2000 materials

**Strengths**
- Passive links melee and ranged damage so both scale together
- Can effectively use both melee and ranged weapons for high total DPS
- Great synergy with flat damage items (Hedgehog, Handcuffs)

**Weaknesses**
- Limited to 3 melee + 3 ranged weapons, so weapon mistakes hurt more
- Needs careful stat allocation, over-investing one side is inefficient
- Hybrid setup often has a tighter defensive stat budget

**Signature build** Ghost Weapon Generalist (Ghost Axe/Scepter/Flint)

---

## Loud
**Unlock** Kill 5000 enemies

**Strengths**
- High early damage from +30% Damage secures kills and level-ups fast
- More enemies (+50%) synergize with on-kill scaling and economy items
- Great with high-attack-speed, lifesteal-focused melee for Endless scaling

**Weaknesses**
- Permanent -3 Harvesting per wave punishes slow starts and greedy economy
- Extra enemies plus low starting defenses make him fragile without armor/dodge/lifesteal
- Needs strong wave control and positioning at higher difficulties

**Signature build** Speed Demon Ghost Flint (6x Ghost Flint)

---

## Multitasker
**Unlock** Collect 5000 materials

**Strengths**
- Can equip up to 12 weapons for huge coverage and on-hit synergies
- Innate +20% Damage, scales well with % damage, melee damage and crit
- Great with weapons that reward many copies (Stick) or on-hit procs (Lightning Shiv)
- Flexible itemization, can spread stats without losing much power

**Weaknesses**
- Loses 5% Damage per weapon equipped, feels weak without aggressive damage stacking
- Harder to keep weapon tiers high while managing up to 12 slots
- Can be fragile at high difficulty if Max HP/armor/sustain are ignored
- Reroll and combine management gets complex, punishes newer players

**Signature build** 12 Stick Build (up to 12x Stick)

---

## Wildling
**Unlock** Kill 10000 enemies

**Strengths**
- Huge lifesteal bonus with Primitive weapons makes sustain easy
- Starts with a Stick, transitions well into Primitive setups
- Synergizes with risky self-damage items like Blood Donation thanks to sustain
- Beginner-friendly, high survivability and simple stat priorities

**Weaknesses**
- Cannot equip weapons above tier 2, capping peak DPS and boss damage
- Relies heavily on Primitive weapons (Stick, Torch, Slingshot) for full value
- Needs strong damage scaling to compensate for lower weapon tiers
- Many healing/regen items are low value compared to pure damage scaling

**Signature build** Lifesteal Slingshot Wildling (6x Slingshot)

---

## Pacifist
**Unlock** Collect 10000 materials

**Strengths**
- Passive material and XP income from every living enemy at wave end
- Starts with a Lumberjack Shirt for easy tree farming and drops
- Can scale economy extremely hard without ever needing to kill
- Excels at a survival, kite-and-outlast playstyle

**Weaknesses**
- -100% Damage makes traditional offensive builds nearly impossible
- -100 Engineering rules out structure-based damage or utility
- Relies heavily on positioning, kiting and defensive stats to survive crowds
- Very item- and stat-dependent, a weak early game snowballs badly

**Signature build** Ultimate Pacifist (6x Hand, pure economy/kiting)

---

## Gladiator
**Unlock** Kill 20000 enemies

**Strengths**
- Massive attack speed scaling for every different melee weapon equipped
- Flat +5 Melee Damage gives a strong early game
- Flexible weapon pool as long as you stay in melee

**Weaknesses**
- Starts with -40% Attack Speed and cannot use ranged weapons
- -30 Luck makes early shop rolls and item drops worse
- Builds require juggling many different weapons, harder to optimize

**Signature build** Multi Weapon Gladiator (Spear/Rock/Hatchet/Stick/Torch/Cacti Club)

---

## Saver
**Unlock** Collect 20000 materials

**Strengths**
- Built-in Piggy Bank plus +15 Harvesting make a powerful economy engine
- +1% Damage per 25 materials heavily rewards saving for huge late-game damage
- Pairs extremely well with HP/material-scaling items like Padding

**Weaknesses**
- +50% item and weapon prices make the early game fragile, punishes greedy rerolls
- Power spikes are delayed, can feel underpowered until you've banked a lot
- Requires spending discipline and good positioning to survive while saving

**Signature build** Spear Saver / Super Saver (hoard materials, Slingshot or Thief Dagger)
