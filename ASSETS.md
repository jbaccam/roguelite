## Beach Cove kit (map two) — September 22, 2026

Twenty-five original Blender assets for the Beach Cove arena: ten grass-capped stone headlands and plateaus, five bare shoreline rocks, three palms, driftwood, driftwood planks, a wrecked rowboat, two tide pools and two grass patches. Source, generator, FBX/GLB exports, texture sheets, real Blender renders and round-trip validation are in `roguelite-planning/blender-beach-cove-kit/`. No Creator Store model is used.

Every asset is modelled at its final Roblox stud size with the painted texture repeat held constant, so the kit imports at 1:1 and must not be scaled. This is the fix for the stretched textures caused by upsizing the map-one cliff kit in Studio; see that folder's README. Assets are sized for a 125-stud arena, matching the user's existing Studio map.

The folder also assembles the arena: `build_arena.py` places 116 instances into the reference composition and writes `arena-layout.json`, and `emit_studio_script.py` turns that into `BuildBeachCove.server.luau`, which rebuilds `Workspace.BeachCoveArena` from MeshParts the user imports into `ServerStorage.BeachCoveKit`. Cliffs are scenery; collision comes from an invisible boundary ring.

Stone, grass, the grass-to-stone transition, fronds, palm bark and water are hue/value regrades of the user's own hand-painted mountain sheets, supplied September 23, 2026 as the quality bar for this kit and recoloured for the beach so the two maps do not read as the same cliffs. Driftwood is a regrade of the existing log bark sheet. Sand and boat planking are authored procedurally. All originals are copied unchanged into `textures/source/` with SHA256 recorded in `texture-provenance.json`; none were modified. The user-supplied beach reference (`ChatGPT Image Sep 22, 2026, 07_47_05 PM.png`) is preserved unchanged as `textures/source/beach-cove-reference.png`. No source image was modified.

All 25 exports pass Blender round-trip validation. Roblox Studio import and in-game behaviour have not been tested for this kit.

## Faithful hammer-zombie rebuild (art study) — September 23, 2026

A fresh Blender recreation of the same supplied hammer-zombie reference, in
`roguelite-planning/hammer-boss-faithful/`. Model, pose and baked textures only:
no rig, no animation, no FBX, nothing uploaded or installed. It does not replace
the boss running in the roguelite; `hammer-boss/finished/` remains the installed
asset, and the earlier `hammer-boss-rebuild/` draft is left untouched.

Landmarks were measured off the sheet at 0.01078 studs per pixel rather than
estimated, and material colour was tuned by sampling five regions of the render
against the same regions of the reference until every material agreed within
2-12% per channel. The face is authored artwork rasterised in the generator and
projected onto shallow relief; no pixels from the supplied image are used. The
reference remains preserved unchanged at `hammer-boss/source/boss-reference.png`
with the SHA256 already recorded there.

Verified in Blender only: the generator runs clean under 5.2.1 LTS, the 4K atlas
bakes, and nine renders plus five side-by-side comparison sheets were produced
and inspected. Roblox Studio import and in-engine appearance are untested, and
the asset has no rig. Known gaps from the reference are listed in its README.
