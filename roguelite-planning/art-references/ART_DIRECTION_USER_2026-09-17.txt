The game uses a stylized low-poly 3D aesthetic with soft faceting, clean silhouettes, chunky proportions, painterly textures, and bright but controlled colors. The overall visual language should feel somewhere between a polished Roblox game, a mobile action game, and a hand-painted fantasy toy world.
The style should be immediately readable from a distance, simple enough for Roblox performance, but still detailed enough to feel intentional and high quality.
The world should never look realistic, gritty, hyper-detailed, or physically accurate. It should feel playful, slightly exaggerated, colorful, and cohesive.
Everything in the game should follow these principles:
- low-poly but polished 
- chunky and readable silhouettes 
- soft bevels instead of razor-sharp edges 
- faceted geometry is visible 
- textures provide visual richness 
- geometry provides shape 
- bright but not neon-everywhere 
- stylized painterly materials 
- simple color palettes 
- slightly exaggerated proportions 
- minimal realistic detail 
- game-ready, Roblox-friendly assets 
- clean presentation 
- consistent world scale 
- consistent texture scale 
The style should not look like:
-  realistic PBR 
-  photorealism 
-  Minecraft 
-  voxel art 
-  pure flat-shaded geometry 
-  overly smooth Pixar-style models 
-  highly detailed fantasy MMO assets 
-  hyper-detailed sculpted meshes 
-  plastic toy models with no texture variation 
The ideal middle ground is:
simple geometry + strong silhouette + subtle painterly texture + clean faceting
Models should be visibly low-poly, but not primitive.
Use:
-  moderate polygon counts 
-  broad planar surfaces 
-  slightly irregular faceting 
-  subtle bevels 
-  rounded/chamfered corners 
-  asymmetry where natural 
-  simplified forms 
Avoid:
-  huge triangular facets dominating surfaces 
-  perfectly smooth subdivision 
-  razor-sharp 90° edges 
-  excessive tiny geometry 
-  random noisy topology 
-  hyper-detailed sculpting 
Edges should usually be slightly softened.
For hard-surface objects:
-  beveled edges 
-  chunky simplified forms 
-  major shapes clearly readable 
-  limited small details 
For natural objects:
-  irregular silhouettes 
-  slight tapering 
-  asymmetrical mass 
-  softened polygonal edges 
Textures are one of the most important parts of the style.
They should be:
-  painterly 
-  stylized 
-  soft 
-  low-noise 
-  slightly hand-painted 
-  broad color variation 
-  subtle surface detail 
Avoid:
-  realistic photographic textures 
-  extreme dirt 
-  micro-scratches everywhere 
-  high-frequency noise 
-  sharp photoreal cracks 
-  heavy PBR detail 
Textures should contain:
-  2–4 main value ranges 
-  gentle color variation 
-  broad patches 
-  subtle grunge 
-  occasional highlights 
-  hand-painted-looking shading 
For most assets:
-  roughness: fairly high 
-  metallic: only when appropriate 
-  normal maps: optional, subtle 
-  displacement: generally unnecessary 
The base color texture should do most of the visual work.

Large 500x500 Roblox surfaces should not reveal obvious texture repetition.
