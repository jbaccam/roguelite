Armor clarity: what armor does and where to see it

Play-test: "I'm a little confused what armor does and where to see it." Mockup only. Dashed lime outlines mark what is new; everything else is today's screen. Shown at 1920 × 1080 and on an 844 × 390 phone.

Two things are called armor. The Armor stat cuts the damage of every hit, and players never do the sum: every screen shows it as a percent, "Armor 8 · you take 35% less damage" (negative: "Armor -3 · you take 20% more damage"). Each point does a bit less than the one before: 5 Armor = 25% less, 10 = 40%, 15 = 50%, 30 = 67%. Anything that adds Armor shows the change from your Armor now: "+2 Armor (25% → 32% less damage)". Armor pieces (from chests) do nothing alone except add their tier to Gear Power; 2 of one set give its bonus and all 4 add its perk.

Pause screen (P in a run): ARMOR group + Armor in plain words

Example: Brawler (+3 Armor), Pot Lid (+2), Iron helmet IV + chest II + legs I (2-piece on), one Ninja boot. Armor 7 = you take 32% less damage; the Iron 2-piece line shows its +2 as 25% → 32%. Each worn set says ON or how many more pieces; the numbers are the real tier-scaled ones. Contact resistance and First-hit blocks show under DEFENSE only when you have them. The shop and level-up stat lists get the same Armor label ("Armor · you take 32% less damage"); shop item cards (Pot Lid etc.) and the Armor level-up card add "(25% → 32% less damage)".

Armory, Armor tab: explainer, WEARING line, bonus status

Two short lines over the slot filter (how sets work; your Armor as a percent with a 5 / 15 / 30 reference), then what you wear now. The detail panel's 2 / 4 PIECES rows show ON or WEAR n MORE, and one line says what a single piece does (Gear Power). The rest of the panel moves down 84; it still fits 810 tall and scrolls on phones as before.
