Item card from the Legendary Chest odds

Every item in the chest's odds list (and the Pet Egg's pet list) can be tapped. It opens a card that shows the item's big icon, its rarity, its stats at every tier (I–IV), its special in plain words, whether you own it and at what tier, and the chance to get it from that chest. Close it with X, BACK, the B button, Esc or by tapping outside. Each state is drawn at PC size (1920×1080) and on an 844×390 phone (shown at its real size: the game draws on a 1500×738 canvas at scale 0.5 inside the phone's safe area).

Every number and sentence part comes from the game modules, run in Studio and saved to data.json, never typed by hand. Ownership uses a sample profile (Katana Tier II, Phoenix Helmet Tier I, Baby Dragon Tier I).
