## Stage contract

`Scene.new(parent, origin, player)` returns a scene or `nil, reason`. `origin.Y` is aligned with the center's actual grass ray hit. `scene.groundY`, `scene.presenter`, `scene.dummies`, `scene.island`, `scene.ground`, `scene.folder` and `scene.lane` describe the prepared scene. Use the existing ArmoryStage capability set for this UI module, including its permitted Humanoid display properties.

Actors expose `model`, `root`, `rest`, `footOffset`, `bounds`, `boundsCenter`, `scene`, `visible` and `hit`. Dummies additionally expose `target` (the actual textured mesh) and `highlight`. `Scene.place(actor,x,z,facing)` accepts coordinates relative to the scene origin, rays the real grass, and sets the actor's feet 0.015 studs above it. Facing may be a flat direction Vector3, CFrame orientation, or yaw. It updates `actor.rest` to the root CFrame, which equals the model pivot; Stage may safely call `model:PivotTo(actor.rest)` during recoil. `Scene.repositionRoot` applies transient poses without replacing rest. `Scene.setVisible` controls only local transparency. `scene:destroy()` is idempotent.

The supported central lane is x±18, z±28. The initial presenter stands at (0,12), facing −Z; default targets face +Z. Keep active targets at least five studs away from the presenter and use a smaller three-target group for most attacks. Bounds support camera framing with the avatar's real body size; the scene does not rescale the player or the reviewed dummy.
