# Armor kit: Iron (Common)

**Status: Blender-verified, Studio untested.** Nothing here has been imported into Roblox Studio or checked in Play.

A sturdy young squire's kit: cool brushed-steel plates over a dark brown leather undersuit, mid brown leather for belt,
straps and boots. No gold, gems, glow, spikes, scales, particles or lights. Fitted to the normalized R15 body
(`../r15-proxy/*.obj`, read only) and generated by one self-contained script, `build_iron.py`.

**Cover pass (2026-09-30):** the owner did not like the tan quilted cloth showing on the shoulders (front and back) and
behind the legs. Steel now covers those areas and the undersuit is dark leather; quilting survives only as a thin trim at
the collar and wrist cuffs. The before sheet is kept in `previews/before-cover/sheet.png`.

## Design
- **Helmet (`"open"`)**: rounded kettle / nasal helm. Dome standing 0.07 stud off the skull (room for hair), one raised
  ridge front to back, a rolled brim, a short nasal bar, 4 rivets. Face shows.
- **Chest**: one rounded breastplate with a soft centre ridge and a rolled neck edge, plus a plainer back plate. Both now run
  out to 0.96 of the torso half-width and up onto the shoulder line, so no cloth shows between them and the shoulder
  plates. The back plate is taller (neck edge at 0.74, it rises to the shoulder only outside |u| 0.5 so it stays clear of
  the head) and covers the shoulder blades and upper back. Two plain abdomen bands lapping over each other, a leather belt
  with one square steel buckle.
- **Arms**: upper arm = rounded shoulder plate, a new **rerebrace** (steel plate round the front, outer and back, from under
  the shoulder plate to the lame, with one leather strap and 4 rivets; the inner face stays clear; it stops well above the
  elbow) and the flared lame under it. Lower arm: plain vambrace with one leather strap and a thin tan quilted cuff below it.
- **Legs**: plain thigh plate (front and outer side) plus a **separate back lame** wrapped round the back of the thigh
  (2 rivets); shin greave with a rolled hem and one strap, plus a **calf guard** round the back of the shin (soft calf
  bulge; the strap rides over it); rounded knee cop over the thigh plate. Back lame stops 0.1 above the knee plane and the
  calf guard 0.1 below it, so the back of the knee stays open and can bend. Nothing on the inner leg faces.
- **Boots**: brown leather boot with a steel toe cap, dark sole strip, and a rolled leather cuff on the lower leg.
- **Undersuit**: dark brown leather (texture only: grain, faint seams, baked AO). Quilted gambeson (tan, painted diamond
  pattern) is now only a thin collar strip at the neckline and a thin cuff strip under each vambrace.
- **Detail**: about 55 five-sided rivets in neat rows at plate edges, 1-segment bevels with painted edge highlights,
  baked AO in every overlap, light scuffed-edge wear.
- **Palette**: steel (light grey, blue-grey shadows), leather brown, dark undersuit brown, muted tan trim.

## Rebuild
```
"C:/Program Files/Blender Foundation/Blender 5.2/blender.exe" -b --factory-startup --threads 4 --python build_iron.py
"C:/Program Files/Blender Foundation/Blender 5.2/blender.exe" -b --factory-startup --threads 4 --python validate_exports.py
```
About 30 s (bake at 1024, 8 samples). `QUICK=1` = flat colours, no bake or export. Also `VIEWS=a,b`, `NOPOSE=1`, `SAMPLES=n`.
Order in the script: build, `clean_mesh` (slivers, triangulate), unwrap, bake, export. **The clean-up must stay before the
unwrap**: run after it, it merged UV corners across islands and smeared thin triangles over the atlas (dark slits on the
helm dome, ragged plate edges). The bake also floods never-written texels with their painted neighbours.

## Studio install notes
- MeshPart with `TextureID` = the atlas in the install data (SurfaceAppearance renders blank in Play here). Material
  `SmoothPlastic`; lighting is baked into the atlas. Weld each mesh to its `body_part`; bbox centre at `offset`, Studio
  part-local axes. Import at 1:1, do not scale.
- Set the helmet mode to `"open"`: hair shows. The dome stands 0.07 stud off the skull, so tall hair accessories will poke
  through it.
- No Neon meshes, no ParticleEmitters, no lights.

## Fit rules used
Plates 0.028 stud off the body, undersuit 0.012. Nothing on inner leg faces (the thigh back lame and calf guard start 0.025
from the inner plane and stop there), no LowerTorso side plates, nothing thicker than the torso side planes (torso plates end at
0.96 of 1.0). Chart corner radius is 0.05 (the proxy has small chamfers). The shoulder dome is checked numerically at build time
(worst arm vertex is at 0.958 of the dome surface, must be under 1). Undersuit sleeves stop at the joint planes (elbow, knee,
hip) so neighbouring sleeves never overlap coplanar. The lowest upper-arm lame flares so the vambrace top slides under it; the
rerebrace hem tucks under that lame. The knee cop rises over the thigh plate. Back lame bottom and calf guard top each sit 0.1
stud from the knee plane. The undersuit at the neck stays below the head, and the back plate's neck edge stays below the head's
underside (checked by the pose numbers: no UpperTorso armour x Head body pairs at rest).

## Known gaps

  run knee, 161 in the walk). The 0.1 stud gaps leave the knee free up to about 35-45 degrees; past that the two back plates
  touch. Shorten the calf guard top (`tg` / 0.28 in `build_lower_leg_legs`) if Play shows it.
- From behind the set now reads as a full plate harness; the only cloth is a thin collar and cuff strip. The baked dark leather
  is deliberately low contrast against the steel.
- Steel still reads slightly blue in the previews because of the preview sky.
- Facets on the helm dome are visible at close range (16 columns).
- The foot's leather is mostly hidden from the front by the toe cap and the cuff.
- The idle 'Foot armour x LowerLeg body' pairs come from the R15 proxy's built-in foot/leg overlap.
