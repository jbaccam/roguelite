# Armor kit: Iron (Common)

**Status: Blender-verified, Studio untested.** Nothing here has been imported into Roblox Studio or checked in Play.

A sturdy young squire's kit: cool brushed-steel plates over a tan quilted gambeson, dark warm leather for belt, straps
and boots. No gold, gems, glow, spikes, scales, particles or lights. Fitted to the normalized R15 body
(`../r15-proxy/*.obj`, read only) and generated by one self-contained script, `build_iron.py`.

## Design
- **Helmet (`"open"`)**: rounded kettle / nasal helm. Dome standing 0.07 stud off the skull (room for hair), one raised
  ridge front to back, a rolled brim, a short nasal bar, 4 rivets. Face shows.
- **Chest**: one rounded breastplate with a soft centre ridge and a rolled neck edge over the gambeson, plus a plainer
  back plate (the game camera mostly sees the back). Two plain abdomen bands lapping over each other, a leather belt with
  one square steel buckle. Upper arm: one rounded shoulder plate and one lame under it. Lower arm: plain vambrace with
  one leather strap.
- **Legs**: plain thigh plate (front and outer side), rounded knee cop over it, shin greave with one strap.
- **Boots**: brown leather boot with a steel toe cap, dark sole strip, and a rolled leather cuff on the lower leg.
- **Detail**: about 45 five-sided rivets in neat rows at plate edges, 1-segment bevels with painted edge highlights,
  baked AO in every overlap, light scuffed-edge wear. The quilt pattern is texture only (no geometry).
- **Palette**: steel (light grey, blue-grey shadows), leather brown, muted tan gambeson.

## Rebuild
```
"C:/Program Files/Blender Foundation/Blender 5.2/blender.exe" -b --factory-startup --threads 2 --python build_iron.py
"C:/Program Files/Blender Foundation/Blender 5.2/blender.exe" -b --factory-startup --threads 2 --python validate_exports.py
```

About 35 s (bake at 1024, 8 samples). `QUICK=1` = flat colours, no bake or export. Also `VIEWS=a,b`, `NOPOSE=1`, `SAMPLES=n`.

## Studio install notes
- MeshPart with `TextureID` = the atlas in the install data (SurfaceAppearance renders blank in Play here). Material
  `SmoothPlastic`; lighting is baked into the atlas. Weld each mesh to its `body_part`; bbox centre at `offset`, Studio
  part-local axes. Import at 1:1, do not scale.
- Set the helmet mode to `"open"`: hair shows. The dome stands 0.07 stud off the skull, so tall hair accessories will poke
  through it.
- No Neon meshes, no ParticleEmitters, no lights.

## Fit rules used
Plates 0.028 stud off the body, gambeson 0.012. Nothing on inner leg faces, no LowerTorso side plates, nothing thicker
than the torso side planes. Chart corner radius is 0.05 (the proxy has small chamfers). The shoulder dome is checked
numerically at build time (worst arm vertex is at 0.958 of the dome surface, must be under 1). Gambeson sleeves stop at the
joint planes (elbow, knee, hip) so neighbouring sleeves never overlap coplanar. The lowest upper-arm lame flares so the
vambrace top slides under it. The knee cop rises over the thigh plate. The gambeson at the neck stays below the head.

## Known gaps
- From behind, the legs read as padded trousers: only the front and outer thigh, and the shin, have plates, per the brief.
  A calf guard, dark leather trousers or a tasset skirt would fix this if wanted.
- Steel still reads slightly blue in the previews because of the preview sky.
- Facets on the helm dome are visible at close range (16 columns).
- The foot's leather is mostly hidden from the front by the toe cap and the cuff.
- The idle 'Foot armour x LowerLeg body' pairs come from the R15 proxy's built-in foot/leg overlap.

## Provenance
Everything is generated procedurally by `build_iron.py`. No Creator Store or third-party assets. The fitting proxy is the R15
body measured in Studio on 2026-09-28 (`../r15-proxy/`).
