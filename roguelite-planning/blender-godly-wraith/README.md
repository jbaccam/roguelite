# Crimson Wraith (Godly Reaper's Scythe helper)

Blender-verified, Studio untested.

The wraith the Reaper's Scythe raises from enemies it kills. It flies at enemies and claws them for 6 seconds.

## Design

- **Silhouette:** a floating hooded reaper-wraith. The big pointed hood sweeps back at the peak. The broad, hunched shoulder shroud carries bone spikes. Two long bony arms end in oversized hooked claws. The body tapers into a ragged tail with no legs. Seen from the front it makes a strong V; seen from behind (the game camera) it shows the hood, the spikes and the glowing tail.
- **Face:** the scythe's skull language. An angry V brow, slanted hex sockets with crimson eyes and white-hot pupils, and an open jaw that juts forward, with fangs and a glowing throat.
- **Chest:** the shroud opens in a V with crimson-lined edges. Inside, hooked ribs curl around a glowing soul core.
- **Claws:** four talons per hand (three plus a thumb). Each is diamond-section with a sharp keel and a needle tip, and they darken toward the points.
- **Tail:** a star-section tail with sharp ridges, plus six sharp tatters. Crimson Neon lines run along the tatter edges and down four tail ridges.
- **Scythe links:**
  - the same hooded skull;
  - a thin gold rim on the hood opening;
  - the gold medallion with the glowing eye-rune on the back of the cowl;
  - the bone-spike spine;
  - the same crimson Neon (255,22,52) and hot core (255,150,128).
- **Not the Frost Ghost:** only crimson, near-black and bone. There is no blue and no white sheet-ghost shape.
- **Pose:** a neutral hover, with the arms forward and down and the claws open. This is the rest pose the game animates from.

| **Total** | | **4,996** |

- **Size:** 4.5 studs from the hood tip to the tail tip, with the claws spread 3.93 studs (overall width 4.16).
- **Origin:** Blender (0,0,0) is the body axis at chest height (the hover pivot).
- **Arms:** each arm rotates about its shoulder origin. The arm root is a cloth ball centred on the pivot, hidden inside the shroud, so ±40° swings open no gap. `previews/ClawPose.png` shows a 35° forward-down swipe from the front and from behind.
- **Texture:** one 512 `textures/BaseColor.png`, a painterly bake (noise ramps, AO, cavity, bevel edge highlights, cool shadows and warm lights, crimson glow bleed) shared by the three textured meshes. Use `MeshPart.TextureID`, not SurfaceAppearance.
- **Placement data:** `studio-install-data.json` holds per-part centres and sizes, the shoulder pivots and the key points (eyes, core, tail tip, claw tips) in Studio axes, plus VFX notes.

## Files

- `build_wraith.py`: the self-contained generator.
  - `MODE=quick` runs flat-colour review shots.
  - `MODE=full` (the default) bakes, exports, writes the reports and renders the previews.
- `validate_exports.py` re-imports the FBX and GLB and writes `validation-report.json`.
- `wraith.blend`, `exports/fbx/Wraith.fbx` (texture embedded), `exports/glb/Wraith.glb`, `polygon-report.json`.
- `previews/`: `Preview`, `Front`, `Side`, `Hero` (Cycles, dark stage, bloom), `ClawPose` and `Scale` (beside a 5-stud block figure).

## Provenance

- All geometry is procedural, generated by `build_wraith.py` in Blender 5.2 headless.
- No Creator Store models, no downloaded assets and no user-supplied images were used.
- Helper code (the bmesh primitives, bake shader, catalogue stage and compositor bloom) was copied from `weapon-models/godly/reapers-scythe/build_reapers_scythe.py`. That file is not imported.

## Not verified

- Studio import, the Neon look, TextureID and in-game scale.
- The arm swing in a real animation.

## Known imperfections

- Under the bright catalogue side light (`Side.png`), the shroud reads dark maroon. In `Preview` and `Hero` it reads near-black with a crimson cast.
- The mouth glow can read a little like a red tongue.
- The neck bone shows as a thin stick under the jaw.
- The game-camera view (about 18 studs behind and above, 70° FOV) was only checked in the flat-colour pass. There the wraith is about 120 px tall in a 720p frame, and the hood, spikes, back medallion and glowing tail read clearly.
- `Hero.png` was re-rendered from `wraith.blend` after the framing fix, with the same settings that are now in `build_wraith.py`.
- Non-manifold edges are expected because the separate parts overlap by design. There are 0 loose vertices and 0 zero-area faces (see `validation-report.json`).
