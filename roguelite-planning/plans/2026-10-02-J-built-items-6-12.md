## Review

An independent review (fresh agent) of all plan J commits found four real problems; all are fixed in this record's commit:

1. **Rejoin kept no weapons.** The stash read the weapon slots in ShopService's `PlayerRemoving`, but CharacterService and the combat script tear the slots down in their own `PlayerRemoving`, in no fixed order. Now every shop publish keeps a copy of the slots, and the stash uses the live slots if they're still there, else that copy.
2. **Play Again after the last member disconnected** would have restarted the old run (its wave, builds and shards), because a disconnect doesn't reset the run. Play Again now always starts from a clean reset.
3. **Dropping out and rejoining reset the revive price** (and gave a second Phoenix rise). The run's revive count and Phoenix use now carry over a rejoin of the same run.
4. **`WaitForChild('LegendaryFX')` without a timeout** in LegendaryMoves and LegendaryVisuals would have stalled the combat scripts in a place with the modules but not the remote. Both now look it up without blocking.

Checked clean by the review: the LegendaryFX argument order between server, visuals and sounds; the VfxKit calls; revive pricing (a client can't pay less; a cheaper direct prompt is caught by the purchase checks); the leaderboard save merge; RunAnalytics flushing; the ShopService hooks; no load-time errors.
